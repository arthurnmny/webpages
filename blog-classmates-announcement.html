Introducing the Classmates Wall

📅 August 19, 2026 👥 BHS Class of 2006

We're thrilled to announce the launch of the Classmates Wall — a brand new way to connect with your fellow Bainbridge High School graduates before, during, and after our 20-year reunion!

What's the Classmates Wall?

The Classmates Wall is an interactive digital gallery featuring custom yearbook cards created by members of the Class of 2006. Think of it as a modern twist on the classic yearbook — a place where you can:

  • Create your own yearbook card with your name, current location, a favorite quote, and a profile photo
  • Share your story with updated information, social media handles, and links to your latest accomplishments
  • Browse classmates' profiles and reconnect with friends you haven't seen since graduation
  • Find contact information for classmates you want to reconnect with before the reunion

How to Create Your Card

Getting started is easy! Here's what you need to do:

Step 1: Visit the Classmates Wall

Head over to our Classmates Wall page and click the "Submit Your Card" button.

Step 2: Fill Out Your Profile

Provide your information including:

  • Your full name
  • Current location
  • An updated yearbook quote or fun fact
  • Your Instagram handle (optional)
  • LinkedIn profile URL (optional)
  • Email address (kept private)
  • Links to articles, projects, or work you're proud of (optional)
  • A profile photo

Step 3: Preview Your Card

As you fill out your information, see a live preview of how your yearbook card will look. It features a custom flip animation — hover to see your profile information and contact details!

Step 4: Submit for Approval

Once you're happy with your card, submit it for approval. Your profile will appear on the wall shortly after our team reviews it to prevent spam.

Ready to Create Your Card?

Head to the Classmates Wall now and share your story with the Class of 2006. Let's catch up before the reunion!

Why We Built This

As we approach our 20-year reunion, we wanted to create a way for classmates to reconnect and share what they've been up to since graduation. The Classmates Wall is more than just a directory — it's a celebration of where we all are now, how far we've come, and the connections that define our class.

Whether you're staying local, living abroad, starting a new business, raising a family, or on an entirely different path, we want to hear from you. Your story matters, and the Class of 2006 is excited to reconnect with you.

See You on the Wall!

We can't wait to see your card on the Classmates Wall. Don't be shy — share your story, reconnect with old friends, and help make our 20-year reunion unforgettable.
